# Content Source Rules

## Preserve factual operating content from First Trip

Preserve and migrate verified:
- accommodations
- accommodation prices
- room types
- meal plans
- trips
- trip prices
- transfer prices/rules
- package composition
- booking policies
- customer-facing conditions
- partners
- community/testimonial content that is real

## Rewrite

Rewrite First Trip-specific branding/copy into WEEMAP voice.

Examples that must not be blindly migrated:
- slogans based on the words "First Trip"
- company-name references
- metadata
- CTA language where it is generic or outdated
- old brand story

## Never invent

- reviews
- ratings
- years of operation
- guest totals
- trip totals
- partner counts
- certifications
- prices
- availability
- accommodation amenities
- destinations
- founder/history facts

## Tone

- concise
- confident
- warm
- local
- exploratory
- non-corporate
- non-cheesy
